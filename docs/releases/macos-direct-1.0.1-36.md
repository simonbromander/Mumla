# Mumla Mac Beta 1.0.1 (36)

- Choose System, Light or Dark in Settings or directly from the menu bar's
  Appearance menu. The selection is saved on this Mac.
- Light uses off-white surfaces, graphite type and orange tactile keys. Dark
  keeps the graphite recorder. System follows macOS, including automatic changes.
- The same choice applies to the main window, sheets and floating recorder or
  transcript pill, without changing dictation, hotkeys or auto-paste behavior.
- New Rams-inspired icon: off-white recorder face, charcoal waveform and an
  orange record key.

## Installation

In direct Mac build 35 or later, choose Check for Updates from Mumla's menu or
Settings. Otherwise quit older Mumla copies, unzip the download and move Mumla
to Applications, replacing the previous copy. Do not run multiple copies.

Requires macOS 14 or later; Apple silicon and Intel are supported. Signing team
and app identity are unchanged. Local history, dictionary and downloaded models
are not migrated. Existing microphone, Input Monitoring and Accessibility
permissions remain necessary; this update does not bypass macOS security.

## What to Check

Switch between all three appearances, close and reopen Mumla, then dictate
with another editor focused. Check both the recording pill and the manual-copy
fallback. A confirmed auto-paste should still dismiss the pill.

This is a beta. Automated tests and notarization do not establish physical
hotkey, target-editor paste or live updater/relaunch acceptance on your Mac.
