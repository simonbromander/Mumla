# Code Of Conduct

Mumla welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Be respectful, patient, and specific. Critique code and ideas, not people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and publishing
another person's private information are not acceptable. Respect consent and
privacy when sharing recordings, transcripts, diagnostics, and screenshots.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.
Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close interactions, or restrict
participation. Decisions should be proportionate and protect affected people.

For a conduct concern, contact maintainer
[@simonbromander](https://github.com/simonbromander) to arrange a private channel.
Do not publish sensitive details to obtain contact information.
