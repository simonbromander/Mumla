LS-EEND speaker diarization
Base model: Di Liang and Xiaofei Li, Westlake University.
CoreML conversion: GradientDescent2718; optimized distribution: FluidInference.
Mumla uses the optimized DIHARD III 100 ms CoreML model. It has not retrained it.
