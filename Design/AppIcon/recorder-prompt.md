# Recorder Icon

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Source: `mumla-recorder-source.png`.
The earlier glass source is retained as a design iteration.

`mumla-recorder-mac-source.png` is the generated transparent macOS variant.
It uses the same artwork, continuous rounded corners, and an 8% transparent margin.
Both variants were resized for the asset catalogs using `sips`.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final iOS and macOS application icon for a private voice dictation app called Mumla. Generate a single premium square icon, straight-on orthographic front view, 1024 square, fills the entire canvas edge to edge with dark graphite finely textured molded housing. Restrained industrial design in the spirit of a beautifully crafted small field recorder, functionalist Dieter Rams and Teenage Engineering influence. The central motif is one deeply recessed horizontal pale sage-green monochrome LCD window (no letters or numbers) containing a bold dark abstract audio waveform made of five vertical bars of varying heights, legible at tiny app-icon size. Under the display, precisely centered, a single large round charcoal mechanical record key with a small muted vermilion-red circle inset into its face. Thin machined bevels, subtle material grain, soft light from the upper left, controlled realistic depth, careful alignment and generous spacing, extraordinary finish. Palette only graphite charcoal, pale sage green, one small restrained red record accent. No rainbow, no glassmorphism, no gradients as decoration, no surrounding environment, no phone mockup, no multiple icons, no letters, no text, no logo text, no watermark. All corners of the square image must be fully opaque graphite; the OS will mask the outer shape. Actual icon artwork not a screenshot.
